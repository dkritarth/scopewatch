Watch the gateway decide.

Pick a scenario and press Play. Each action an agent asks for goes through policy, the reasoning auditor, human approval, and the executor in that order. When an action is held, you choose whether to approve it.

ⓘ Recorded, not simulated. Every decision, audit verdict, and execution receipt below was produced by the real Scopewatch gateway when it seeded these scenarios with the offline mock provider (scripts/export_site_demo.py). Your browser replays the record and never decides anything itself. Approve and Deny only show what the gateway would do next. All data is synthetic.
